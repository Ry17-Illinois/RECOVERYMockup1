description: A black-and-white silent documentary film by Alexander Hammid and Maya Deren recording a female cat and the birth and maturation of her five kittens. The film follows the mother cat as she approaches labor, gives birth, nurses her kittens, and cares for them through their early learning and growth, with occasional appearances by a tom cat. The film contains no humans.
